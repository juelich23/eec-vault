Where the inventory sells

Six channels, six different jobs

Rarity goes to auction, where competitive bidding finds the ceiling. Liquid, repeat-traded cards go to marketplaces, where the turn is faster and the comps are public. And some inventory never reaches a platform at all — it sells across a table at a show, or by direct message. Nothing is listed in one place by default.

Alt Marketplace

Collector-native platform for graded cards, pairing auctions with instant offers priced against its own market index. Settlement is quick and the buyer base is specialist rather than general.

Operations

How a card moves through the business

The same four stages every time, from the show floor to settled proceeds. Capital turns over rather than sitting in inventory.

Stage 1 of 4

Acquire

Private purchases, collector trades, and floor buying at national and regional shows including the National Sports Collectors Convention.

Stage 2 of 4

Authenticate

Raw cards are submitted to PSA or Beckett to be graded and encapsulated. The grade is what establishes resale value and makes the card tradeable sight-unseen.

Stage 3 of 4

Place

Each card is assigned to the venue built for it — auction where rarity needs bidders, marketplace where liquidity matters more than ceiling.

Stage 4 of 4

Settle

The platform remits proceeds after the sale clears, and capital is recycled directly into the next acquisition.
